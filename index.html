    Field guide

    How to play

    1. Add one name each. It can be a person, a place, or anything else. Names stay hidden.
    2. Pass the phone in order. Player 1 goes first. Look at your own card for 3.5 seconds, then pass it on.
    3. Give clues. Find the spy. Everyone sees the same name except one randomly chosen player, who sees “SPY”. The spy tries to guess the name.
    4. Make your guesses. When you’re ready, reveal the answer and play again.

    Duplicates are allowed. The spy might be the person who entered the picked name. Names stay on this device and disappear when you reload.
